⭐ Proactive Plant Care

Preventing disease is easier than treating it. Here are some general tips:

1. Promote Good Airflow

Prune plants and give them space. Good air circulation helps leaves dry quickly.

2. Water Wisely

Water at the base of the plant, not on the leaves. Water in the morning.

3. Practice Good Sanitation

Remove and destroy any dead or diseased leaves. Clean your pruning tools.
